04 / Decision checkpoints

What if sales stay low?

Day 10 · Cash shortage forecast

Reduce planned spending and negotiate payment timing before cash runs out.

Day 20 · Offers produce weak returns

Stop unprofitable promotions and focus on direct customer follow-up.

Day 30 · Cash out still exceeds cash in

Reduce the operating scale and reassess the next month’s funding needs.

Make decisions using collections, payment obligations and margins, not sales volume alone.
